Questions

Can my manager read my private chats?

No. A private chat between two people is seen by those two only, not even by the company owner. The management dashboard shows numbers only and never reads a message.

Does the app track my location all day?

No. The location is only taken when you check in or out. Each person sees their own record; managers see their teams'.

Some of our workers have no smartphone. How do they check in?

A supervisor adds them by name and job, then records their check-ins and check-outs from their own phone with a roll call, and the record shows who recorded them. If they join later with an account, their earlier record is linked to them.

Who owns the files we upload?

Work files belong to the company and stay with their project, even if the person who uploaded them leaves.

Can I work with more than one company?

Yes. One account can join several workspaces, and you switch between them from the top of the screen.

How do we pay?

In the app, the company owner asks for a plan and how long they want it. An invoice arrives in their country's currency with how to pay, and the plan is turned on once the payment is confirmed.

What happens when a subscription ends?

You get a reminder a week before it ends and a week's grace after it. Then the workspace goes back to the free plan, and nothing is deleted.
